### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** at **Softix Technologies**, specializing in building high-concurrency enterprise web platforms and integrating production-grade Artificial Intelligence solutions. From architecting end-to-end government investigation case management systems to deploying real-time Computer Vision models in agriculture, I turn complex business challenges into elegant, resilient products.

- 🔭 **Enterprise Architecture:** Building mission-critical, role-based workflows, automated case triage, and reporting engines.
- 🤖 **Applied AI & CV:** Integrating real-time image diagnosis, machine learning pipelines, and conversational LLMs into production.
- 🌐 **Full-Stack Mastery:** Developing responsive, pixel-perfect frontend experiences backed by robust, scalable backend architectures.
- ⚡ **Engineering Principles:** Performance-first optimization, maintainable clean code, and intuitive UX design.
- 🚀 **Open to Opportunities:** High-impact Full-Stack, AI Engineering, and Enterprise Consulting roles.
